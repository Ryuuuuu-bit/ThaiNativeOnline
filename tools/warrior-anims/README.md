# นักรบ (warrior) model and animations

`warrior-tripo.glb` is the warrior and his twin swords from Tripo (generated by the owner
from `docs/art/classes/warrior.webp`), prepared in Blender:

1. warrior decimated from 1.9 M to 28 k triangles, textures 1024²;
2. skinned to the Muay Thai fighter's Tripo skeleton (`tools/muaythai-anims/fighter-tripo.glb`):
   the T-poses match (height 0.98, arm span 0.78 vs 0.81), so the armature is used as is and
   the vertex weights are transferred from the fighter's body (Data Transfer, nearest face
   interpolated);
3. the swords (one Tripo mesh holding the mirrored pair) are split in two, decimated to
   4.5 k triangles each (textures 1024²), scaled to 0.48 of the body height with the grip
   centre as origin, and parented to `mixamorig:LeftHand` / `RightHand`: grip through the
   fist, blade out of the thumb side (T-pose: forward), edge toward the knuckles;
4. exported (JPEG textures) with the fighter's source clips (walk, run, jab …), which play unchanged.

```sh
npm i --no-save @gltf-transform/core@4 @gltf-transform/functions@4
node tools/warrior-anims/compose.mjs tools/warrior-anims/warrior-tripo.glb public/models/warrior.glb
```

`compose.mjs` is the hunter's procedural pose builder (`fighter()`: hinge limbs, leg and arm
IK, palm roll split into the forearm) with one addition: a hand can be given a `blade`
direction instead of a palm. The hand rolls so the thumb (the blade) points that way; what
the blade leans toward the forearm's line is applied after the fist closes, split between
the wrist (35 %) and the grip (65 %, the sword node turns in the hand).
Clip list and timings: `docs/art/classes/warrior/ANIMATIONS.md`.
Portrait (`public/ui/portraits/warrior.png`): Eevee render of the full-resolution model.
Skill icons: PixelLab 48×48 art in `tools/icons/source/warrior/`, framed by `tools/icons/normalize.py`.
