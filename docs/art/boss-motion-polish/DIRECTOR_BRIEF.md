# Boss motion polish

Use the approved Thai Meshy bodies and improve their motion through the real
game controller. All twelve primary map bosses need preparation, a held pose
during the warning, release only on the authoritative impact, and recovery.
The server's footprints, damage, cadence and warning durations remain the
authority. Cancellation, defeat, map changes and repeated packets must not
leave a pose stuck or replay an old strike.

Eight humanoid rigs may use restrained upper-body motion from Meshy's actual
animation library. Select Idle (0), Charged Spell Cast (125), Charged Spell Cast
1 (126), and Right-hand Sword Slash (219). Transfer rotations through measured
rest axes onto the existing rigs. Preserve the generated bodies, painted
textures, weights, feet, hands/fingers, tails and rigid weapon/wing ownership.
Keep humanoid library motion away from the Buffalo, tree spirit, Pu Som and
limbless Naga rigs. Their existing species-specific clips remain appropriate.

Give the Giant and mine guardian a slower preparation and heavier release;
the Commander a disciplined right-handed sword motion; the Garuda a lighter
casting rhythm; the spirits and Rift demon a sustained ritual; and the Naga a
grounded coiling breath. Do not add root motion that moves server collision or
make a character slide through a telegraph. Original model files and their
prior acceptance receipts remain unchanged. Ship optional motion files with
a safe fallback to the original clips if loading fails.

Use one Blender process and one review page. Review front, side, back, top and
the real 2.5D angle, plus desktop and touch layouts. Inspect both hands, elbows,
knees, soles, actual sword/club grip and blade direction. Numerical edge,
limb-length and floor checks complement visual review. Art Bible Style,
Readability and Technical Usability must each reach 8 in the reviewed scope.

No generated animation is accepted solely because Meshy returned success.
Reject or reduce any transfer that breaks anatomy or obscures safe areas.
Record actual Meshy task IDs, selected action IDs, credits, asset hashes and
the sampled motion times; exclude credentials and signed asset URLs.

Additional user scope: show a distinct boss-lair badge on both the current-map
atlas and minimap. Include a searchable boss category, name, level, activation
conditions and CH1 restriction in the atlas details. Use existing boss spawn
sites and monster definitions, independently of discovery or current life
state. A lair is public location information, not a claim that the boss is
currently alive. Selecting a badge must not begin walking until the player
chooses the existing navigation action. Keep city safe, map boundaries correct,
and touch controls reachable.
