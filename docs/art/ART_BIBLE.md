# Thai Native Online — Art Bible

## Core Style
Stylized 3D Thai fantasy MMORPG.
Orthographic / near-orthographic 2.5D.
Anime-influenced characters.
Soft hand-painted environment feeling.
The target is not realism.

## Visual Pillars
1. Clear silhouettes
2. Soft stylized shapes
3. Strong gameplay readability
4. Charming fantasy atmosphere
5. Thai identity through selective details
6. Consistency over asset quantity

## Environment
Use clustered vegetation, simplified large shapes, clear paths, readable rocks, layered foliage, soft terrain transitions.
Avoid photoreal foliage, noisy textures, empty flat terrain, excessive micro-detail.

## Character
Readable from gameplay camera first.
Use strong hairstyle, costume color blocks, class silhouette, readable weapons.
Avoid realistic proportions and tiny detail that disappears at gameplay scale.

## Lighting
Soft directional light, readable shadows, bright enough ambient fill.
Avoid crushed blacks, harsh cinematic contrast, excessive bloom.

## Thai Identity
Use architecture, clothing, weapons, vegetation, mythology, props, and patterns selectively.
Do not cover every surface in ornament.

## Approval Gate
Art Director review target:
- Style Match >= 8/10
- Readability >= 8/10
- Technical Usability >= 8/10
