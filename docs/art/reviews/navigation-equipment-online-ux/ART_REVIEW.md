The camera-relative navigation arrow, route distance and manual arrival/status
wording retain the existing green/gold Thai palette. The inspected narrow touch
placement clears controls; the journal remains available when no edge gap fits.
The equipment table separates current and projected values, retains signed
benefits/losses, and exposes conditional cards rather than promising fixed damage.

An initial classic parchment review required revision (readability 7/10): pale
labels, disclosure headings and deltas lacked contrast. Scoped selected-detail
ink corrections now show dark labels, headings, metadata and notes, with dark
green/orange signed deltas. Fresh [desktop classic comparison](desktop-classic-comparison-table.png)
and [touch scrolled actions](touch-equipment-1-actions.png) pass. The inspected
equipment decomposition and dark hover tooltip remain readable; actions are
reachable after scrolling. No required visual changes remain.

Independent [browser QA](../../../technical/qa/navigation-equipment-ui.md)
records all nine captures, four contrast fixture sets (minimum 4.7607:1 across
the sampled actual texture/gradient), and six classic action/tooltip/pointer
checks. These are QA measurements, not measurements performed by Art Director.

Scope: supplied changed UI captures only. Unrelated existing HUD overlaps,
arbitrary dynamic camera/nameplate states and physical-device performance are
not certified. Authenticated persistence is reviewed separately in the
