# Living city — final art review

**Status: approved.** Review covers the final production geometry, actual Game
stills and sampled motion frames 00/16/32/47 from market, temple and waterfront.

| Criterion | Score |
| --- | --- |
| Style match | 8/10 |
| Readability | 8/10 |
| Thai identity | 9/10 |
| Technical usability | 8/10 |

The temple's decorated entrance establishes Thai identity at normal gameplay
zoom 2. The full monumental roof extends beyond this close frame; the temple
quarter review camera shows the complete silhouette. The market has visible
resident travel while keeping the fountain and service approaches readable.
Its composition remains spacious. Pier, boat and water separate clearly; the
previous milky, repeating water highlights are resolved. Environmental movement
is restrained rather than exaggerated.

No further visual source changes were requested. The final diagnostic recapture
has zero browser errors and replaces earlier captures with stale diagnostic HUD
timestamps or Vite-only socket warnings. Production code did not change for this
diagnostic correction.

Sampled frames do not establish animation smoothness or hardware FPS. Soundscape
weights and audio continuity were measured, but music quality, stereo balance
and audible transitions have not received a listening review. These scores are
an internal art assessment, not user approval or a performance benchmark.
