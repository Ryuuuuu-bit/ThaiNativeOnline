Monster families should be tied to biome, progression, and gameplay role.

Each zone should normally include:
- passive/ambient creature
- normal melee threat
- ranged or control threat
- aggressive variant
- elite
- boss

Do not create monsters only because they look cool. Every creature needs a gameplay purpose, readable silhouette, scale target, and habitat rationale.
